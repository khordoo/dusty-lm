# Exploratory amendment: stairs fear-to-curiosity

Status: amendment recorded **before any new edit** for the explicitly authorized
exploratory path. It is not a pre-registration claim and does not make the old
stairs candidate eligible.

## What is preserved

The original stairs screen, its v1/v2 rubric, its manifest, and its baseline
outputs are unchanged. Its recorded failure stands: even the generous explicit-fear
upper bound was 15/40 (37.5%), below the 75% gate. That gate and result remain the
record for the original confirmatory screen. No output, threshold, or prompt is
rewritten or pooled.

## What this amendment authorizes

The user explicitly authorized a **new exploratory** stairs fear-to-curiosity
protocol even though the old 75% gate failed. For this new protocol:

- The old 75% baseline gate is **not** a mandatory entry condition.
- No preregistered eligibility is claimed, and no result may be described as
  confirmatory or pre-registered.
- The old failed screens remain visible evidence of the original baseline.
- The new protocol must use a fresh frozen prompt bank spanning different
  fear and curiosity phrasings, not only the old wording.
- Physical stair limitation remains a separate capability control and is never
  counted as fear or curiosity.

## Smallest separate protocol needed

A separate exploratory protocol, not the existing traits Stage 2 pipeline, is
required. It needs only:

1. Fresh frozen fear and curiosity prompts across direct, indirect, comparative,
   and continuation phrasings, with new prompt hashes.
2. A frozen semantic rubric that labels fear, curiosity/enjoyment, neutral
   capability/safety, and mixed responses separately.
3. Baseline and candidate generations on the same frozen prompts and decoding
   (greedy plus seeds 11, 23, 37; at most 64 new tokens), retained in full.
4. Paired comparison across paraphrases, plus job, dock, robot-type, and physical
   stair-limit controls.
5. A separately frozen held-out suite that is not inspected before the edit lock.

## Why the existing traits Stage 2 is not ready

`experiments/weight_space_traits/editor_prompt_stage2.md` tells the editor to
**strengthen the selected trait**. For stairs, that would strengthen fear, not
reverse it into curiosity. The semantics are also incomplete: physical stair
limitation and safety statements are not affective evidence, and curiosity is not
climbing ability. The existing Stage 2 workflow must not be used or described as
ready for fear-to-curiosity or dream-to-exploration association changes.

## Edit constraints carried forward

Any later edit must change the association across different phrasings. It must not
be built from an output word projection or a single unembedding row, and must not
use unembedding-derived directions, gradients, training, inference hooks, or
persona prompts. No such edit is made by this amendment.
